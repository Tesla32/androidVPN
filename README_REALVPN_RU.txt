Cloavy VPN Android Real VPN Test v2

Kak testirovat:
1. V wg-easy nazhmi Download u klienta cloavy-android-test i skachai .conf.
2. Peredai .conf na Android telefon.
3. Otkroi proekt v Android Studio.
4. Zapusti prilozhenie na telefone.
5. Na glavnom ekrane nazhmi Import v kartochke Real VPN test.
6. Vyberi .conf fail.
7. Nazhmi glavnuju knopku Protect / Zashchitit.
8. Podtverdi sistemnoe VPN-razreshenie Android.
9. Prover IP v brauzere.

Vazhno:
- QR i .conf ne otpravlyat v chat.
- Vnutri .conf est private key.
- Eto testovaya sborka, ne produkshen.
